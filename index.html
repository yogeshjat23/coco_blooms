Good to know

How do I order?

Tap Order on WhatsApp on any bouquet, or send the contact form. Share your colours, card message and the date you need it, and we will confirm the details and final price.

Can I change the colours or flowers?

Yes. Every piece can be made in your favourite colours. Custom designs may vary in price.

Do the flowers really last forever?

Pipecleaner flowers do not wilt and need no water. Keep them dry and dust them gently, and they stay pretty for years.

Can you add a message card?

Yes. We can add a handwritten note, like the Happy Birthday card on our Evil Eye bouquet.

Do you make keychains and flower pots?

They are coming to the shop soon. Message us on WhatsApp and we can make one for you today.
